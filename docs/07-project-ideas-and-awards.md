# 💡 Project Ideas & Award Categories

## The 10 Award Categories

Alongside the internship shortlist, there are **ten award categories**. Each award is worth **₹50,000**.

> No individual or team can win more than one award. Awards are decided independently of internship selection.

## Award 09: 🎬 Demo Day Drop Award
**The best 60-second demo video.**

This is about **presentation quality**, not project complexity. **Any project can win this** with a great video.

**Tips for winning:**
- Hook in the first 5 seconds — show the problem dramatically
- Keep it to exactly 60 seconds
- Show the app WORKING, not slides
- Use real voice input, not typed text
- End with impact — what problem did you solve?
- Good audio quality in the video itself

---

## Award 10: 🗳️ Crowd Favourite Award
**Decided by public votes on LinkedIn and X using the challenge hashtags.**

This is about **genuine engagement** on your social media posts.

**Tips:**
- Tell a relatable story (everyone knows kirana stores)
- Make the video shareable — would you share it?
- Post at peak hours (8-10 AM or 6-8 PM IST)
- Engage with every comment
- Share in relevant communities (college groups, tech communities)
- Ask friends and family to share (not fake engagement — genuine sharing)
