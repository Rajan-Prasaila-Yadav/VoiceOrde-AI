# 👤 User Personas

> These are example personas to design for, not real people.

---

## Persona 1: Ramesh, the Shopkeeper

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Runs a kirana store in a small town; about 45; uses a smartphone, WhatsApp and UPI |
| **Language** | Hindi and a regional language; little English |
| **Daily pain** | Phone orders arrive during the evening rush; he writes items on paper and adds the bill in his head |
| **Wants** | A correct bill fast, fewer mistakes, a record of who owes what |
| **Fears** | Wrong quantity or price costs him money; learning something complicated |
| **How VoiceOrder AI helps** | Speak or tap record, check the screen, confirm, send the bill |
| **Success looks like** | An order takes under a minute and the total is always right |

---

## Persona 2: Sunita, the Regular Customer

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Homemaker; about 35; buys groceries from the same shop every week |
| **Language** | Hindi or a regional language; some Hinglish |
| **Daily pain** | Does not want to browse an app or type long lists |
| **Wants** | To say the list once and be sure it is right |
| **Fears** | Wrong item delivered, wrong amount charged |
| **How VoiceOrder AI helps** | Speaks the order, hears it read back, confirms, tracks it |
| **Success looks like** | Gets the right items and a clear bill without calling again |

---

## Persona 3: Delivery Helper (Kept Simple)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | A delivery person or helper at the shop |
| **Needs to see** | The order list, the address, and a **Delivered** button on a phone |
| **Does not need** | Pricing details, catalogue access, or customer history |
