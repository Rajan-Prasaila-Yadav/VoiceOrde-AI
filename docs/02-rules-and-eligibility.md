# 📋 Rules & Eligibility

## Eligibility Criteria

You must meet **all three** conditions to participate:

| # | Requirement | Details |
|---|------------|---------|
| ✅ | **Student Status** | Currently enrolled in a degree or course at a college or university in India |
| ✅ | **Age** | 18 years or older on the day you register |
| ✅ | **Team Size** | Building solo, or with one teammate who also meets these conditions |

### Email Requirement

- You **must** enrol with your **institutional email** on the Gnani AI platform
- If you **don't have** an institutional email, drop your **student enrolment details** and **LinkedIn profile** to `internshipscontest@gnani.ai` to be verified

| Rule | Details |

## The Rules That Matter Most

### 1. Offers and Stipends Are Individual
- Teammates are interviewed **separately**
- One can receive an offer **without the other**
- Stipends of up to ₹1 lakh/month are paid **per intern**, never split

### 2. The Brief Is Open
- There's **no set problem statement**
- Build any working application on Gnani AI's models or APIs

### 3. Your Project Uses Gnani AI Technology
- Your submission must **name the Gnani AI models or APIs** it uses
- Must describe **each member's contribution**

### 4. You Demo It in Public
- Post your project on **LinkedIn or X**
- Use hashtags: `#GnaniAI` and `#GreatIndianAIInternshipChallenge`
- **Nominate those posts** in your submission
- The post has to be **live before you submit**

### 5. The Deadline Is Fixed
- **11:59 PM IST on November 10, 2026**
- You can edit project details until you submit

### 6. Keep It Authentic
- No bought engagement, bots, paid promotion, or engagement swaps
- No plagiarism or misleading demos
- Entries with substantiated breaches may be **disqualified**

## Solo vs Team of Two

### Solo
- You register, build, and submit on your own

### Team of Two
- You register and add your **one teammate's email**
- Teams **can't be larger than two**
- 5,000 credits go to the **primary registrant's** account
- All contest communication goes to the **primary registrant**
- Each teammate is **interviewed and considered individually**
- Your teammate accepts the terms independently

> [!WARNING]
> For teams: Gnani AI keeps the teammate's email on record **only** to verify eligibility and participation. All credits and communication go to the primary registrant.
